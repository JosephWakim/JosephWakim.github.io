Chromo

Chromo is a Monte Carlo simulator used to study the physical factors that influence chromatin organization. Simulations with Chromo reveal how the cooperative binding of reader proteins drives 3D genome organization.

[Docs] [GitHub] [PNAS 2025] [PNAS 2024]

ConfinedWLC

ConfinedWLC is a Monte Carlo simulator used to study how wormlike chains (WLCs) organize under tight spherical confinement. Simulations with ConfinedWLC reveal how properties of the polymer and its confinement influence the pressure exerted on the confining boundary.
